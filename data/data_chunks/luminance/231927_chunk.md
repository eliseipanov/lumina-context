The lighting strategy involved two key approaches. First, a simulated 'high altitude natural light' with hard sun source + ambient toplight. Second, a practical 'DIY studio' setup mimicking the lead character's map-making: tungsten Chinese lanterns for soft ambient fill, a blue-LED desk lamp for moonlight simulation, and LED fairy lights as stylized streetlights. A unique solution was integrating diffusion filters into practical lamp shades (on the camera side) to compensate for the 5D's limited dynamic range. The color temperature contrast between warm tungsten (2800K) lanterns, cool blue LED moonlight (5600K), and soft fill was used to enhance the 'toy-sized world' metaphor. Hard-source tracking car shots contrasted with the soft, ambient-rich map sequences.

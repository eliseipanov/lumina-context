Key framing decisions included central composition of moving cars in wide shots to maximize bend-turn space, and extreme close-ups for the map's miniature details. The jib-mounted camera used sweeping arcs over the map surface, creating dynamic perspective shifts as it 'hovered' over the terrain. Fixed focus framing maintained consistent depth relationships between characters and environment. The 50mm lens (equivalent to 32mm on Super 35) created compressed spatial relationships in map scenes, contrasting with the wide-angle framing of real-world car tracking shots. Nighttime sequences used soft focus transitions between practical lights and background map elements to enhance the 'magical domesticity' theme.

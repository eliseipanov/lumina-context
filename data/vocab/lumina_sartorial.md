## [Axis: Sartorial]
> **Definition**: The layer of clothing, accessories, and body adornments. It defines the social status, professional belonging, or the total absence of artificial layers (nudity).

### Sub-axis: Dress State & Coverage
- **nude**: Total absence of clothing; focus on pure anatomy, skin texture, and biological form.
- **partial_nudity**: Subject is partially covered (e.g., draped fabric, underwear, or strategic posing).
- **lingerie_swimwear**: Intimate or functional minimal clothing; focuses on silhouette and skin.
- **clothed**: Standard everyday attire; covers the body according to social norms.
- **layered_outfit**: Complex dressing with multiple garments (e.g., coat over a suit with a scarf).

### Sub-axis: Style & Era
- **period_piece**: Historical garments (e.g., Victorian corsets, Medieval tunics, Roman togas).
- **contemporary_casual**: Modern everyday clothing (jeans, t-shirts, hoodies, sneakers).
- **formal_wear**: Elegant attire (tuxedos, evening gowns, ceremonial robes).
- **tactical_functional**: Workwear, military gear, or technical outfits (uniforms, high-vis, armor).
- **avant_garde**: Experimental, sculptural, or high-fashion silhouettes that defy standard forms.

### Sub-axis: Fabric & Detail
- **draped**: Fabric that hangs loosely, emphasizing gravity and the body's movement.
- **form_fitting**: Clothing that tightly follows the contours of the anatomy.
- **weathered_apparel**: Signs of wear on clothing (tears, stains, fading, repairs).
- **ornate**: Highly detailed garments with embroidery, jewelry, or complex patterns.
- **translucent_fabric**: Sheer materials (lace, silk, thin linen) that partially reveal what's beneath.

### Sub-axis: Accessories & Adornments
- **headgear**: Hats, helmets, veils, or crowns.
- **jewelry**: Functional or decorative metal/gemstone additions (piercings, necklaces, rings).
- **body_art**: Permanent or temporary modifications (tattoos, ritual paint, scarification).

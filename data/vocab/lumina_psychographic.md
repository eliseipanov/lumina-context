## [Axis: Psychographic]
> **Definition**: The internal world, emotional state, and soul of the subject. It defines the "vibe," temperament, and psychological depth that manifests through physical cues.

### Sub-axis: Emotional State (The "Now")
- **contemplative**: Deep in thought, looking inward; a sense of stillness and reflection.
- **intense**: Fierce focus, high emotional pressure; eyes that "pierce" the viewer.
- **melancholic**: A sense of gentle sadness, longing, or nostalgia; "heavy" eyes.
- **joyful**: Radiating positive energy; genuine warmth in the gaze and facial muscles.
- **stoic**: Emotionally controlled, resilient, and unshakeable; a "poker face" with hidden depth.
- **vulnerable**: Exposed, raw, and fragile; a sense of openness that invites empathy.

### Sub-axis: Archetypal Vibe (The "Soul")
- **noble**: Inherently dignified, graceful, and authoritative; a "royal" or high-born essence.
- **rebellious**: Defiant, non-conformist, and raw; a spirit of challenge and disruption.
- **mysterious**: Guarded, enigmatic, and layered; suggests secrets hidden beneath the surface.
- **sinister**: Distrustful, menacing, or morally ambiguous; a "dark" or unsettling presence.
- **innocent**: Pure, naive, and untouched by cynicism; a clear and honest gaze.

### Sub-axis: Physical Mimicry & Micro-expressions
- **subtle_smile**: A faint, barely-perceivable twitch of the lips (Mona Lisa effect).
- **furrowed_brow**: Concentration, worry, or suppressed anger.
- **distant_gaze**: Focus is fixed on something far away or non-existent in the frame; "thousand-yard stare."
- **micro_tension**: Subtle muscle tightening around the jaw or eyes; indicates hidden stress or resolve.

### Sub-axis: Atmosphere & Resonance
- **ethereal**: Otherworldly, light, and spiritual; as if the subject is not fully "here."
- **gritty**: Raw, hardened, and weathered by life; a sense of "dirt under the fingernails" and survival.
- **cozy**: Warm, inviting, and safe; a domestic and peaceful psychological state.
- **tense**: High anxiety, suspicion, or "fight-or-flight" readiness.

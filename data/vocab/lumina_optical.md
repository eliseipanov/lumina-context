## [Axis: Optical]
> **Definition**: The physics of observation, spatial geometry, and lens-based characteristics that define how the "Universe fragment" is captured.

### Sub-axis: Perspective & Field of View
- **wide_angle**: Broad perspective (14mm-35mm), exaggerates distance between foreground and background; may cause edge distortion.
- **standard_view**: Natural human-like perspective (50mm); minimal distortion, realistic proportions.
- **telephoto**: Compressed perspective (85mm-200mm+), brings background closer to the subject; flattens the scene.
- **fisheye**: Extreme ultra-wide distortion; hemispherical or wide panoramic mapping of the space.
- **anamorphic**: Cinematic widescreen stretch; oval bokeh and horizontal lens flares.

### Sub-axis: Depth & Focus
- **shallow_dof**: Very thin plane of sharpness; background and foreground are heavily blurred (bokeh).
- **deep_focus**: Large depth of field; everything from the closest object to the horizon is sharp.
- **rack_focus**: A shift in focus within a scene (or a prompt implying a specific point of interest).
- **soft_focus**: Intentionally diffused sharpness; creates a dreamy, ethereal, or vintage glow.
- **macro_view**: Extreme close-up on tiny details, often with ultra-shallow depth of field.

### Sub-axis: Camera Position (Observer)
- **eye_level**: Observer is at the same height as the subject; neutral and direct.
- **bird_eye_view**: Observation from high above, looking straight down; minimizes subjects, emphasizes layout.
- **worm_eye_view**: Observation from a very low angle, looking up; makes subjects appear heroic, towering, or imposing.
- **dutch_angle**: Tilted horizon line; creates a sense of unease, tension, or psychological instability.

### Sub-axis: Artifacts & Physics
- **motion_blur**: Streaks or blurring caused by movement of the subject or the observer.
- **lens_flare**: Light scattering caused by bright sources hitting the "lens" elements.
- **chromatic_aberration**: Color fringing at high-contrast edges; adds a raw, optical, or vintage feel.
- **film_grain**: Texture of physical film or sensor noise; defines the "physicality" of the medium.

## [Axis: Composition]
> **Definition**: The structural arrangement of visual elements within the frame. It dictates balance, rhythm, and the viewer's path through the image.

### Sub-axis: Framing & Scale
- **full_body**: The entire subject is visible from head to toe, including the environment immediately around them.
- **close_up**: Focus on the head and shoulders; emphasizes facial detail and emotions.
- **extreme_close_up**: Focus on a single detail (an eye, a hand, a texture); fills the entire frame.
- **wide_shot**: Subject is small relative to the environment; emphasizes scale and context.
- **cowboy_shot**: Subject captured from the mid-thigh up (classic cinematic framing).

### Sub-axis: Geometry & Balance
- **rule_of_thirds**: Key elements are aligned with the intersections of a 3x3 grid.
- **centered**: Subject is placed exactly in the middle; creates symmetry and a sense of "iconic" presence.
- **leading_lines**: Geometric paths (roads, walls, rays) that guide the eye toward the subject.
- **symmetry**: Perfect balance between left/right or top/bottom halves of the frame.
- **negative_space**: Large "empty" areas that emphasize the isolation or importance of the subject.

### Sub-axis: Perspective & Depth
- **foreground_interest**: Objects placed very close to the lens to create a sense of depth and "entry" into the scene.
- **layered_composition**: Distinguishable foreground, midground, and background planes.
- **vanishing_point**: Parallel lines receding into the distance to a single point (classic linear perspective).
- **over_the_shoulder**: Looking at the subject past the silhouette or edge of another person/object.

### Sub-axis: Dynamic Layout
- **diagonal_flow**: Elements arranged along diagonals to create energy and motion.
- **triangular_composition**: Grouping elements into a triangle for stability or strength.
- **cluttered**: Intentional density of objects; creates a sense of chaos, richness, or "lived-in" space.
- **minimalist**: Maximum reduction of elements; focus on pure form and color.

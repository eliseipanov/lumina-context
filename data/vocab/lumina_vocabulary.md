# Lumina Mandatory Vocabulary v1.0 (English)

## [Axis: Luminance]
- **hard_light**: Harsh shadows, sharp transitions. Triggers: direct sun, bare flash.
- **soft_light**: Soft gradients, diffused shadows. Triggers: softbox, clouds, bounce.
- **high_key**: Bright tones dominate, minimal deep shadows.
- **low_key**: Dark tones dominate, deep shadows, high contrast.
- **rim_light**: Backlighting that separates the subject from the background.
- **practicals**: Light sources visible within the frame (lamps, candles, screens).

## [Axis: Optical]
- **anamorphic**: Oval bokeh, horizontal blue flares, widescreen perspective.
- **shallow_dof**: Low depth of field, strong background blur (bokeh).
- **deep_focus**: Entire frame is sharp from foreground to background.
- **wide_angle**: Broad field of view, exaggerated perspective, edge distortion.
- **vintage_lens**: Optical imperfections, softness, and flares typical of old glass.

## [Axis: Somatic]
- **contrapposto**: Weight on one leg, shoulders and hips twisted in opposite directions.
- **open_pose**: Uncrossed limbs, torso facing the camera, approachable stance.
- **closed_pose**: Crossed arms/legs, guarded or defensive posture.
- **eye_level**: Direct gaze at camera height, eye-to-eye contact.
- **dynamic_tension**: Feeling of motion in static pose, tensed muscles, unfinished gesture.

## [Axis: Material]
- **weathered**: Textures with signs of wear, dirt, rust, or aging.
- **specular**: Strong mirror-like reflections on surfaces (metal, wet pavement).
- **translucent**: Semi-transparent materials (backlit skin, thin fabric, leaves).

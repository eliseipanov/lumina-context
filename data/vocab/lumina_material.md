## [Axis: Material]
> **Definition**: The physical properties, textures, and surface qualities of non-biological matter and the environment. Focuses on how light interacts with substance.

### Sub-axis: Surface Finish & Physics
- **matte**: Non-reflective, light-absorbing surface (e.g., dry concrete, unpolished plastic, charcoal).
- **specular**: Highly reflective, mirror-like quality (e.g., polished chrome, wet pavement, gloss finish).
- **translucent**: Semi-transparent; allows light to pass through but scatters it (e.g., frosted glass, thin wax, jade).
- **weathered**: Signs of physical aging, erosion, or wear (e.g., rust, peeling paint, cracked stone, oxidation).
- **polished**: Smooth, refined, and often shiny surface (e.g., varnished wood, buffed marble).

### Sub-axis: Elemental Textures
- **organic_non_living**: Natural materials that are no longer part of a living organism (e.g., raw wood, cut stone, dried soil).
- **metallic**: Qualities of metal (e.g., brushed steel, hammered copper, rusted iron, gold leaf).
- **mineral**: Crystalline or earthy textures (e.g., obsidian, quartz, sand, gravel).
- **synthetic**: Human-made textures (e.g., carbon fiber, polymer, reinforced concrete, nylon).

### Sub-axis: Liquid & Fluid
- **viscous**: Thick, slow-moving liquids (e.g., oil, honey, melting wax, tar).
- **aqueous**: Water-like properties; clarity, ripples, and surface tension.
- **crystalline**: Transparent and structured (e.g., ice, diamond, glass shards).
- **gaseous**: Textures of air and particles (e.g., thick smoke, mist, steam, smog).

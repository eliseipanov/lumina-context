## [Axis: Luminance]
> **Definition**: The physics and qualities of light, including intensity, shadows, color temperature, and specific light sources.

### Sub-axis: Light Quality
- **hard_light**: Harsh, well-defined shadows with sharp transitions. (e.g., direct midday sun).
- **soft_light**: Diffused, gentle shadows with smooth gradients. (e.g., cloudy sky, softbox).
- **high_key**: Dominantly bright tones, minimal deep shadows; airy and light feel.
- **low_key**: Dominantly dark tones, deep shadows, high contrast; moody and dramatic.
- **specular_highlights**: Bright, mirror-like reflections on surfaces (wet skin, metal, glass).

### Sub-axis: Direction & Setup
- **rim_light**: Backlighting that creates a bright outline around the subject, separating them from the background.
- **top_light**: Light from directly above; creates deep shadows in eye sockets (butterfly or dramatic lighting).
- **side_light**: Light from the side; emphasizes texture and volume through shadow.
- **backlight**: Source behind the subject; creates silhouettes or glowing edges.
- **golden_hour**: Warm, low-angle natural light (sunset/sunrise).

### Sub-axis: Sources & Primitives
- **practicals**: Light sources visible within the frame (lamps, candles, neon signs, screens).
- **ambient_light**: General, non-directional light filling the scene.
- **volumetric_light**: Light beams visible through fog, dust, or smoke (God rays).
- **bioluminescence**: Light emitted by living organisms or magical/sci-fi organic sources.

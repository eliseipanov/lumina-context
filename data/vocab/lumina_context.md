## [Axis: Context]
> **Definition**: The macro-environment, era, and situational narrative surrounding the subject. It defines "Where and When" the fragment of the Universe exists.

### Sub-axis: Environment & Location
- **outdoors_nature**: Wild spaces (forests, mountains, oceans, deserts) untouched by civilization.
- **urban_landscape**: City environments (streets, rooftops, plazas, alleys, industrial zones).
- **interior_domestic**: Living spaces (bedrooms, kitchens, cluttered workshops, minimalist lofts).
- **interior_institutional**: Public or formal spaces (libraries, hospitals, cathedrals, bunkers).
- **cultivated_nature**: Nature shaped by man (strawberry gardens, parks, zen gardens, vineyards).

### Sub-axis: Chronology & Era
- **prehistoric**: Primordial landscapes, caves, and ancient flora.
- **historical**: Specific eras (Victorian, Medieval, Ancient Rome, 1920s Noir).
- **contemporary**: The modern world, current technology, and fashion.
- **futuristic**: Sci-fi settings, cyberpunk cities, or high-tech space interiors.

### Sub-axis: Narrative & Situation (The "Vibe")
- **conflict_zone**: Signs of war, ruins, smoke, barricades; a sense of danger and urgency.
- **civil_unrest**: Crowds, demonstrations, protest signs; high social energy and tension.
- **solitude**: Isolated settings (a single house in a field, a lonely bench); focus on silence.
- **festive**: Celebration, decorations, crowds in a joyful state, rituals.
- **post_apocalyptic**: Nature reclaiming ruins; a sense of "after the end."

### Sub-axis: Physics & Climate
- **meteorological**: Weather conditions (heavy rain, snowstorm, heat haze, fog).
- **seasonal**: Temporal markers (autumn leaves, spring bloom, winter frost, summer glare).
- **diurnal_cycle**: Time of day (dawn, high noon, dusk, midnight).
- **zero_gravity**: Absence of weight; objects and hair floating, unusual physics.

# Lumina Mandatory Vocabulary v1.1 (English)

## [Axis: Domain]
- **photography**: Real-world captured images, light on sensor/film.
- **anime**: Japanese animation style, cel-shading, distinct line art.
- **digital_art**: Modern 2D/3D illustration, concept art, matte painting.
- **oil_painting**: Traditional fine art texture, visible brushstrokes, canvas feel.
- **drawing**: Pencil, charcoal, or ink sketches; focus on lines and hatching.
- **cinematic_still**: High-end movie production look, specific lighting and grading.
- **claymation**: Stop-motion style, sculpted textures, 3D tactile feel.

## [Axis: Luminance]
- **hard_light**: Harsh shadows, sharp transitions. Triggers: direct sun, bare flash.
- **soft_light**: Soft gradients, diffused shadows. Triggers: softbox, clouds, bounce.
- **high_key**: Bright tones dominate, minimal deep shadows.
- **low_key**: Dark tones dominate, deep shadows, high contrast.
- **rim_light**: Backlighting that separates the subject from the background.
- **practicals**: Light sources visible within the frame (lamps, candles, screens).

## [Axis: Optical]
- **anamorphic**: Oval bokeh, horizontal blue flares, widescreen perspective.
- **shallow_dof**: Low depth of field, strong background blur (bokeh).
- **deep_focus**: Entire frame is sharp from foreground to background.
- **wide_angle**: Broad field of view, exaggerated perspective, edge distortion.
- **vintage_lens**: Optical imperfections, softness, and flares typical of old glass.

## [Axis: Composition]
- **rule_of_thirds**: Subjects aligned with grid intersections.
- **leading_lines**: Lines (roads, walls, paths) directing the eye to the subject.
- **centered**: Subject placed exactly in the middle for symmetry.
- **full_body**: Character visible from head to toe.
- **close_up**: Focus on head and shoulders; high facial detail.
- **bird_eye_view**: Looking straight down from high above.
- **worm_eye_view**: Looking up from a very low angle.

## [Axis: Somatic]
- **gestures**: Detailed hands/fingers expression (pointing, grasping, touching).
- **lying**: Body positioned horizontally on a surface (bed, floor, grass).
- **contrapposto**: Weight on one leg, shoulders and hips twisted.
- **open_pose**: Uncrossed limbs, torso facing the camera.
- **closed_pose**: Crossed arms/legs, guarded or defensive posture.
- **eye_level**: Direct gaze at camera height, eye-to-eye contact.
- **dynamic_tension**: Feeling of motion in static pose, tensed muscles.

## [Axis: Material]
- **weathered**: Textures with signs of wear, dirt, rust, or aging.
- **specular**: Strong mirror-like reflections on surfaces (metal, wet pavement).
- **translucent**: Semi-transparent materials (backlit skin, thin fabric, leaves).
- **matte**: Non-reflective surfaces, light-absorbing (plastic, concrete, dry earth).
- **organic**: Natural textures (wood, raw stone, wool, linen).
- **polished**: Smooth, finished surfaces (varnished furniture, polished marble).

## [Axis: Context]
- **architectural_style**: Building types (gothic, brutalist, modern, industrial, baroque).
- **interior_domestic**: Residential spaces and furniture (living_room, kitchen, mid-century).
- **outdoors_nature**: Natural environments (forest, mountains, coastline, meadow).
- **urban_landscape**: City environments (streets, alleys, skyscrapers, plazas).

## [Axis: Sartorial]
- **period_piece**: Clothing from a specific era (victorian, 1920s, medieval).
- **revealing**: Clothing that emphasizes nudity or the body's silhouette.
- **layered**: Complex outfits with multiple layers, accessories, and draping.
- **functional**: Workwear, uniforms, or tactical gear.

Physical elements included: Ordnance Survey map constructed from found objects (metallic scraps, paper, plastic) to create tactile mixed-media texture; vintage Asahi Pentax-M 50mm/f1.4 lens with passive EF adapter; jib-mounted Blackmagic camera system; Chinese lanterns with tough-spun diffuser in practical shades; LED fairy lights and tungsten lamps as light-modifying materials; 0.45 ND filters and half Soft FX filter modifying light quality. Textural contrast was achieved through: a) weathered map surface vs. smooth camera body; b) warm filament glow of practicals vs. cool digital recording; c) soft diffusion material in lamp shades vs. sharp map details.

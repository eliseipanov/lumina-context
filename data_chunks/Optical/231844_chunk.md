The team used a Canon 5D Mk III paired with an Asahi Pentax-M 50mm/f1.4 vintage K-mount lens (mounted via passive EF adapter). This 50mm focal length on full-frame is equivalent to 32mm on Super 35, matching the principal photography's most-used lenses. A half Soft FX filter was added for diffusion. For day-to-day car tracking shots, a Blackmagic Micro Cinema Camera was mounted with vibration-dampening clamps to a roof rack. Lenses were fixed focus at approx. 6m with variable ND filter adjustments made manually during takes. All map sequences were shot at f1.4 to create shallow depth of field, requiring 0.45 ND filters for daylight exposure control. The camera output was recorded via HDMI to a Blackmagic Video Assist using ProRes 422 HQ.
